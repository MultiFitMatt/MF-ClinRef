Functional Peptide Profile

Sermorelin (GHRH 1-29) is a growth hormone-releasing hormone analogue. Unlike exogenous HGH, Sermorelin stimulates the patient's pituitary gland to produce its own growth hormone, preserving the negative feedback loop and reducing the risk of tachyphylaxis and side effects associated with rHGH.

CLINICAL INDICATIONS (OFF-LABEL)

BODY COMPOSITION

Increases lean muscle mass and lipolysis (fat loss), particularly visceral adipose tissue. Often combined with lifestyle interventions.

SLEEP ARCHITECTURE

Enhances Stage 4 non-REM slow-wave sleep. Patients often report improved sleep quality within the first 2 weeks of therapy.

RECOVERY & REPAIR

Accelerates recovery from musculoskeletal injuries and surgery via increased IGF-1 signaling.

COGNITIVE FUNCTION

Potential neuroprotective effects. Users frequently report improved mental clarity and general sense of well-being.
