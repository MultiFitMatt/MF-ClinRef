PDE5 INHIBITOR CLASS

SILDENAFIL CITRATE

A potent, selective inhibitor of cGMP-specific phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5). While standardly prescribed for ED, functional applications extend to endothelial rehabilitation, pulmonary arterial modulation, and systemic nitric oxide optimization.

Primary Targets

  • Corpus Cavernosum Smooth muscle relaxation via NO/cGMP pathway.
  • Pulmonary Vasculature Reduction of pulmonary vascular resistance.
  • Endothelium (Systemic) Improvement of flow-mediated dilation.

PHARMACOKINETICS & TIMING

Understanding the absorption window is critical for therapeutic efficacy. High-fat meals drastically alter the Tmax and Cmax, a common cause of "treatment failure" in functional protocols.

ABSORPTION LAG

In a fasted state, Tmax is reached within 30-120 minutes (median 60).

PEAK CONCENTRATION

Cmax reduced by ~29% when taken with a high-fat meal.

DURATION WINDOW

Therapeutic window persists for approximately 4 hours. Terminal half-life is 4 hours.

CLINICAL NUANCE & QUIRKS

01
👁

Cyanopsia

"Blue Vision". Sildenafil has 10% affinity for PDE6 (retina) vs PDE5. High doses may cause transient blue tinge to vision.

Reversible. More common at 100mg doses. Advise pilots or night drivers accordingly.
02
🧠

Auditory Shift

Rare sudden decrease or loss of hearing, sometimes accompanied by tinnitus and dizziness.

FDA post-marketing reports suggest temporal association. Discontinue immediately if auditory changes occur.
03
💊

NO Boosters

Synergistic hypotension risk. Patients supplementing heavily with L-Citrulline or Nitrates may experience exaggerated BP drops.

Contraindicated absolutely with organic nitrates (nitroglycerin). Caution with heavy functional NO stacks.
04
💤

Reflux/GERD

Lower Esophageal Sphincter (LES) is smooth muscle. PDE5i causes relaxation of the LES, exacerbating GERD.

Take with plenty of water. Avoid lying down immediately after dosing.

THE "WASHOUT" PERIOD

Tachyphylaxis (tolerance) to PDE5i is debated but observed in clinical practice. Functional dosing often utilizes "Holidays" to reset NO sensitivity.
