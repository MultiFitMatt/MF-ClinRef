Tadalafil / Cialis

Clinical Reference

Class
PDE5 Inhibitor

Functional Profile

Tadalafil is a selective phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5) inhibitor. Unlike Sildenafil (Viagra), Tadalafil boasts a unique pharmacokinetic profile characterized by a 17.5-hour half-life. This "Weekend Pill" property allows for steady-state plasma concentrations with daily low-dose administration, offering 24/7 therapeutic coverage.

Target Indications
ED, BPH (Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms), Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension.
Functional Uses
Endothelial Health, Nitric Oxide Optimization, "Gym Pumps", Estrogen Modulation.

The 5-Day Rule

Due to the 17.5h half-life, it takes ~4-5 days for Tadalafil to fully clear the system (<5%).

2
When to Cycle Off?

If "Back Pump" or myalgia becomes persistent. Take a 5-7 day break to reset.

CRITICAL: Wait 48+ hours after last dose before nitrate administration.

The "Back Pump"

Common

Lower back myalgia due to PDE11 inhibition in skeletal muscle. Often mimics kidney pain.

RX: Taurine (2-3g), Hydration.

Reflux / GERD

Mech

PDE5 inhibition relaxes the Lower Esophageal Sphincter (LES), allowing acid reflux.

RX: Take with food, stay upright.

Blue Vision

Rare

Less common than Sildenafil. Caused by cross-reaction with PDE6 in the retina.

RX: Stop and reduce dose.
